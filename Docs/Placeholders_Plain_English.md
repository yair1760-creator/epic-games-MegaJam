# What art is still missing (plain English)

*Rated PG for Pig, November 15, evening*

## The short version

Most of the game now has real art. A few things are still grey "stand-in" blocks from early development, and a few flat pictures still look rough next to the hand-drawn ones. Making all of them with Meshy would cost about **70 to 90 credits**. We have **846 credits** left (the team shares them).

If we can only do a few, do the first three. They cost about **24 to 30 credits** together and fix the whole final boss fight and the pink romance set. If there is room for one more, add the Director's face in the speech box (6 credits), so he looks the same in both places.

## Still to make, most important first

**1. The Director (the final boss).**
Right now he is made of grey and coloured boxes. He is the main enemy of the last level, so players stare at him the whole fight. If the designer is drawing him, wait for that. If not, Meshy can draw him the same way the boars are drawn: a flat picture that always faces the player. *About 6 to 12 credits.*

**2. The thing the Director throws at you.**
Every shot he fires is a flat red box. It could be a spinning film reel or a crumpled script page instead. *About 6 credits.*

**3. The sides, back and roof of the pink shed (level 4).**
The front of the shed has a nice drawn picture, but if you walk around it, the sides, back and roof are still plain pink and purple boxes. Two repeating pictures (wooden planks and roof tiles) would cover all of it. *About 12 credits.*

**4. The shed doors.**
You can see them through the shed's doorway, and they are still purple boxes. One door picture would fix them. Saar built the doors, so check with him first. *About 6 credits.*

**5. The King Boar's crown.**
It is a rough picture drawn by code, not by an artist. *About 6 credits.*

**6. The health bar above each boar.**
It is a plain red and black strip. A small drawn frame, like the player's own health bar, would match the rest of the screen. *About 6 credits.*

**7. The Director's face in the speech box.**
Every time the Director talks, his little portrait shows next to the words, in every level. It is a simple flat drawing, without the thick ink lines and shading the rest of the game has. Best made at the same time as the boss (item 1), so they look like the same person. *About 6 credits.*

**8. The victory screen ("That's a wrap!").**
It is still a plain flat box. The other screens (the death screen, the ammo counter, the recording strip) got drawn frames yesterday. *About 6 credits.*

**9. The hit and puff-of-dust effects.**
The little star when the sword hits and the dust cloud when a boar dies were drawn by a script, so they are plain shapes with an outline. They show up constantly, so a hand-drawn set would help. A designer would do this best; Meshy can try. *About 6 to 12 credits.*

**10. Boars seen from the side or from behind.**
There is only a front drawing of the boar, so it looks the same from every side. This is really a job for the designer. *About 6 to 12 credits per pose if Meshy does it.*

## All the flat (2D) art at a glance

**Hand-drawn by the team, finished:**
the sword and shotgun in the player's hands (including reloading), the player's death drawings, the boar (front view, all its moves), the Princess, the bacon, the grass, hay bales and apple trees, the game logo, the START / QUIT / RESUME buttons, the PAUSED panel, the player's health bar, and the running-boar loading animation.

**Made with Meshy to match the team's style, finished:**
the main menu background, the OPTIONS button and the crying-boar card, the four main screen frames (health, speech, controls, script), the recording strip, the ammo counter, the death screen, the wanted poster, the butcher stall signs, and all the set cut-outs (flowers, rainbow, PG sign, shed front, clapperboard, meat hooks, windmill sails, boar holes).

**Still rough or missing:**
the Director (missing), the King Boar's crown, the Director's face in the speech box, the victory screen, the health bar over the boars, the hit and dust effects, and side and back views of the boar.

**Fine as they are:** the aiming dot, the heart and the small clapper icons. They are simple, but they are tiny and easy to read.

## Small fixes that cost nothing

These need no new art, just reusing pictures we already have:

- The ground outside the play area is still a flat colour.
- The two white posts holding up the pink "PG" sign.
- The wooden beams behind the big painted backdrop (you only see them from behind).
- The loading screen background is a plain colour. It could reuse the main menu picture.
- The shotgun sound is still Unreal's sample sound. This is a sound job, not a Meshy job.

## Already done today

- **Pine trees:** all 120 are real 3D trees now, each one turned a little differently.
- **Windmill:** new art that matches the village houses. It moves in one piece, and the flickering sails are fixed.
- **Village:** the big tower with the film camera on top, the scaffolding, the boar holes and the backs of the house cut-outs (the "SET 4" writing now reads the right way round).
- **Boss arena:** walls, cover boxes, studio lights and the camera crane.
- **Pink romance set:** the shed's front, flowers, rainbow, heart-shaped PG sign and ribbon posts.
- **Butcher stall and film crew sets:** meat hooks, empty plate, director's chair, clapperboard, camera track, light stands and the props table.
- **Main menu:** an OPTIONS button that shows a crying boar and "not enough budget for the options".
- **Painted backdrop:** the hills now meet the grass, with no gap or stretched sky.

Every grey block that got new art is still hidden underneath it, so walls and objects still block the player exactly as before.
